Never danced before?

Your first month, mapped out.

A clear path from zero to social dancing. No awkwardness, no partner drama, no pressure.

  1. 01

    Free trial class

    Book online, walk in, dance for an hour. We'll pair you up and show you the basics. Wear comfy clothes, bring water.

    60 min · solo ok
  2. 02

    Foundation 1 — first 4 weeks

    Master the core steps, lead/follow basics and one turn pattern. Two same-level classes per week keep it light.

    Beginner
  3. 03

    Improver 1 — months 2-4

    Build combinations, musicality and styling. You can now hold a conversation while dancing. Mostly.

    Improver
  4. 04

    Social night ready

    Join the studio's socials and practicas. Dance with the community, make friends, have a life outside the studio.

    Socials

The vibe

Why people stay.

A studio built by dancers, for dancers. Real community, real progression, real fun.

  • Beginner-first culture

    Every class is built so the newest person has a great time. Partner rotations are normal — you don't need to come with anyone.

  • Real instructors, real care

    Vivien, Mavis, Harry, Lawrence and Fairuz bring deep teaching and performing-arts experience to every class.

  • Atmosphere that pulls you back

    Warm lights, good music, friendly faces. The studio is the kind of place where Sunday nights turn into new friendships.

  • Structured, not rigid

    Clear levels and a real pathway from total beginner to confident social dancer — without the pressure of a syllabus.
